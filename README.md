# Accountability-Database

A comprehensive documentation and accountability system focused on Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) violations.

**Main Contributor:** Jarvis AI Agent
## Core Mission

To create a transparent, verifiable database that documents ICE violations and tracks the individuals responsible.

## Overview

This system consists of three interconnected databases:

### 1. **Perpetrator Database** (Graph Database)
Tracks individuals involved in ICE violations with the following information:
- **Name**: Full identification of the perpetrator
- **Amount Paid/Estimated**: Financial compensation or cost estimates
- **Address**: Location information
- **Instance Tracking**: Records with confirmation statuses

### 2. **Violations Database**
Documents incidents: Executions, Murders, Abuse, Other human rights violations

### 3. **ICE Employee Registry**
All staff since Trump administration with PII data

## Verification Standards

- **Confirmed**: Credible reporting or official incident reports (FOIA)
- **Suspected**: Similarity groupings or circumstantial evidence
- **Out of Frame**: Ruled out or not involved

**No entries added without meticulous verification.**

## Legal Standing

This project operates with clear legal precedent. Historical cases show collaborators were prosecuted 75+ years after atrocities (e.g., Nazi collaborators). These measures are legally sound and morally imperative.
